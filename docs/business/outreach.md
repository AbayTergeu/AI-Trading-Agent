# Outreach

## Current Campaign

Dubai Car Detailing — China Sourcing

## Sender

Personal Gmail — testing phase.

## Daily Limit

Initial test:
5-10 highly personalized emails per day.

No mass mailing.

## Objective

Get the customer to provide ONE product they regularly purchase.

We then:

1. Research Chinese manufacturers.
2. Compare prices.
3. Calculate landed cost.
4. Prepare a quotation.

---

# Email Template 1 — Standard

## Subject

China sourcing for detailing supplies

## Body

Hi [Name],

I came across [Company] while researching professional
car detailing businesses in Dubai.

We are currently building a China-to-UAE sourcing service
for professional detailing businesses.

We can source products such as microfiber towels,
polishing pads, detailing brushes, applicators and
other workshop consumables directly from manufacturers
in China.

If you currently purchase any of these products in volume,
I can check several Chinese manufacturers and send you
a comparison with estimated delivery to Dubai.

Would you be open to testing this with one product
you currently purchase?

Best regards,
Abay
