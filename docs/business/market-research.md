## Business Hypothesis

We help Dubai/UAE detailing businesses source selected
professional consumables directly from Chinese manufacturers.

Instead of selling a fixed catalogue, we:

1. Find customer demand.
2. Identify the required product.
3. Find Chinese manufacturers.
4. Compare suppliers.
5. Calculate landed cost.
6. Prepare quotation.
7. Negotiate with supplier.
8. Close the deal with human approval.

## Key Principle

Customer first → demand → product → supplier.

NOT:

Supplier → product → try to find a customer.
